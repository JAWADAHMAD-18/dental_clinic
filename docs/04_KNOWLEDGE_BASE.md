## New patient first visit
A first visit takes about 60 minutes. It includes a conversation about your goals and concerns, a full oral examination, X-rays if the dentist considers them necessary, and a clear explanation of any recommended treatment and its cost before anything proceeds. Please arrive 10 minutes early and bring your health fund card if you have one.

## Check-up and clean
A routine check-up and professional clean includes an examination, scale and polish, and personalised advice. Many patients visit every six months, but the dentist will recommend how often is right for you. Sample fee: a standard check-up and clean from AUD 195. Final cost depends on what the dentist finds and any X-rays needed.

## Fillings
Fillings repair teeth damaged by decay. The dentist will assess the tooth, explain the options and confirm the cost before starting. Sample fee: from AUD 220 per tooth depending on size and location.

## Children's dental care
We welcome children of all ages. Early visits help children feel comfortable. Children's check-ups focus on gentle examination, cleaning and brushing advice for parents. Eligible children may be covered under government dental schemes; please contact the clinic to check eligibility before booking. Sample fee: children's check-up and clean from AUD 120.

## Teeth whitening
Professional whitening options are offered after an examination to confirm it is suitable for you. Sample fee: take-home whitening kit from AUD 450. Results vary and a dentist will explain what to expect.

## Clear aligner consultations
A consultation covers whether clear aligners may suit your teeth, how treatment works, how long it typically takes and the cost range. Sample fee: consultation AUD 95. Treatment costs are quoted after assessment and are not available through this assistant.

## Wisdom teeth
If you are experiencing problems with a wisdom tooth, the dentist can examine the area and, if needed, arrange X-rays and discuss options, which may include referral to a specialist. This assistant cannot assess symptoms; please contact the clinic.

## Emergency dental appointments
We keep a small number of same-day appointments for urgent dental pain where possible. Call the clinic as early as you can on (02) 5550 0142. If you have trouble breathing or swallowing, spreading swelling of the face or neck, severe bleeding that won't stop, or a serious injury to the face or jaw, call 000 immediately or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
